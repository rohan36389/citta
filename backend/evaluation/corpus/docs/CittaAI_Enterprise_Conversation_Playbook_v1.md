## Representative Conversation (PROD_001)

Customer:
We operate 180 retail stores and are evaluating platforms. What is Ecommerce OS?

Assistant:
E-Commerce OS is intended to help retailers manage commerce operations from a unified environment. Depending on implementation, it can support inventory visibility, order management, customer engagement, and centralized operations. What is your biggest operational challenge today?

Customer:
Inventory visibility across stores.

Assistant:
Thank you. For multi-store retailers, inventory visibility is a common focus area. Are you currently using an ERP such as SAP or Oracle?
